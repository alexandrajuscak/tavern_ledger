Status meaning: `official` = published rules; `playtest` = Unearthed Arcana (never imported as normal official content,
blocked by the campaign content policy unless the DM allows playtest material); `third_party` = licensed or partner
material; `unverified` = a source page whose publication status could not be confirmed (treated like playtest by the
content policy). Campaign custom feats have status `homebrew` and are not part of this report.

Automation meaning: `automated` = every rules effect is applied by the engine; `partially_automated` = the numeric parts
(ability increase, proficiencies, HP, speed, resources, spells …) are applied and the rest is a reminder; `informational`
= selectable and tracked, but the effect is a reminder (situational or table-adjudicated rules).
